### Process lessons

- **Polish epics benefit from strict scope boundaries.** All four stories touched UX only; no domain rule changes. The epic shipped in 8 feature/fix commits with zero merge conflicts.
- **Metadata mutation is a distinct service path.** `updateDictation` as a separate service (not a branch in `dictation-save.ts`) kept snapshot immutability auditable and testable in isolation.
- **Shell-level sticky is the right abstraction.** One change in `dashboard-chrome.tsx` covered all G1 surfaces without per-page CSS duplication.
- **Documentation drift accumulates on amend stories.** 6-2 changed behavior established in 3-2; upstream spec/docs were not updated in-session. `deferred-work.md` caught it but reconciliation deferred.
- **MVP backlog is complete.** Epic 6 was the last planned epic in `epics.md`. Next work requires explicit product decision (post-MVP ideas, new epic, or ship).
